Andy Kim

Product Design Engineer — SharkNinja

I work at the intersection of mechanical engineering and human-centered design, turning ideas into products people actually use. At SharkNinja I contribute to the design and development of consumer products across the Shark and Ninja lines — from early concept through prototyping and production.

My process is grounded in understanding how people interact with objects in the real world. I use SolidWorks for CAD, rapid 3D printing for iteration, and hands-on prototyping to close the gap between what looks good on screen and what works in your hand.
